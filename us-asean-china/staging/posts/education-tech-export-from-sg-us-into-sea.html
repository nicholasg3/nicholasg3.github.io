Edtech export fails on system fit, not only content

US and Singapore edtech playbooks meet different school systems, languages, and device realities across SEA. System fit beats content libraries.

The claim

Edtech exported from Singapore and the US into broader SEA often assumes bandwidth, device access, assessment culture, and payment ability that vary sharply by country and city tier.

Curriculum politics can matter as much as UX.

How to ground it

UNESCO and national education ministry materials bound system structure. Company usage claims need primary confirmation.

Language localization is not optional for K-12 scale.

Operator consequences

B2B school sales cycles are political. B2C tutoring faces consumer protection and advertising rules.

What to do

Pilot in the education system you claim to serve — public school, private, or consumer — with local partners.

Do not treat Singapore pilot success as Indonesia proof.

  • MOE Singapore: Singapore education ministry primary.
  • UNESCO Education: International education systems context.
  • World Bank Education: Ed systems and edtech development secondary.
