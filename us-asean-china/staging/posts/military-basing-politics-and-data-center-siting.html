Data centers sit in security geography, not only power markets

Latency and megawatts do not erase alliance politics, cable security, and local consent. Infrastructure siting is geopolitical product design.

The claim

Data center siting is sold as power, latency, and land. In contested regions it also intersects alliance politics, cable landing security, and local consent.

Treat basing and security politics as scenario inputs for infrastructure bets.

How to ground it

Official defense cooperation statements and national digital infrastructure plans are primaries. Social posts about bases are not.

Subsea cable maps and outage histories matter as much as megawatt pricing.

Operator consequences

A cheap power region with high political risk can be expensive in insurance and customer diligence.

US, Chinese, and local cloud customers will underwrite location differently.

What to do

For any SEA data center investment memo, add a political/security annex next to the power annex.

Do not site solely on latency heatmaps.

Source notes

  • US DoD: US defense cooperation primary hub (high-level).
  • IMDA Singapore: Digital infrastructure policy example.
  • TeleGeography Submarine Cable Map: Widely used cable map secondary for connectivity context.
  • ASEAN: Regional political context; national security decisions dominate.
