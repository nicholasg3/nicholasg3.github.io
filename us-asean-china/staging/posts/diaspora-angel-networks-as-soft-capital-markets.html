Diaspora angels are a soft capital market

Cross-border angel networks move money and trust outside formal market infrastructure. They are powerful — and biased toward the geographies that created them.

The claim

Diaspora angel networks move checks, hiring, and customer intros across borders without looking like formal capital markets.

They are soft infrastructure for SEA–US–China founder graphs — with bias and blind spots.

How to ground it

Evidence is often network-level and qualitative. Use careful case description rather than fake market-size numbers.

Relate to diaspora parallel-economies memo for commercial rails beyond equity.

Operator consequences

Founders get speed and trust. They can also get homogeneous advice optimized for Silicon Valley norms that fail locally.

What to do

Track where your angels add distribution versus where they only add social proof.

Build local angel capacity if your customers are local.
