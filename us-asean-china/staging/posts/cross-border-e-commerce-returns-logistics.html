Returns write the real cross-border e-commerce P&amp;L

Outbound shipping ads ignore reverse logistics, customs, and refunds. Returns are where cross-border e-commerce unit economics go to die.

The claim

Cross-border e-commerce margins die in returns, customs, and reverse logistics — not only in ads.

SEA multi-country sellers discover this after the first viral SKU.

How to ground it

Customs agency rules and major platform seller policies are primaries for process claims.

UNCTAD and national trade stats help size cross-border flows; they rarely show your reverse-logistics cost.

Operator consequences

Payment refunds, FX, and fraud interact with physical returns. Finance and ops must share one playbook.

What to do

Model landed cost including expected return rate by category before you fund ads.

Design return centers regionally; pure ship-from-China-and-hope fails on customer experience.

Source notes

  • UNCTAD e-commerce: Cross-border e-commerce research hub.
  • World Customs Organization: Customs standards context.
  • UPS (example logistics primary): Carrier materials for cross-border shipping products; compare multiple carriers.
  • ASEAN: Regional trade facilitation context.
