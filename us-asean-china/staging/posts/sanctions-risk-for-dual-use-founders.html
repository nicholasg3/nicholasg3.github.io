Dual-use founders inherit sanctions operations work

If your product can serve military, surveillance, or restricted end users, sanctions and export controls are not a legal appendix. They are customer qualification and engineering constraints.

The claim

Startups in autonomy, chips tooling, advanced materials, geospatial, and certain AI systems sit near dual-use lines even when their homepage says commercial only.

A single restricted counterparty can become an existential enforcement event.

Primary maps

OFAC sanctions programs and SDN lists. BIS export controls and entity lists. Partner-country regimes if you operate from Singapore or elsewhere.

Screening tools help. They do not replace knowing your end use and end user.

Go-to-market redesign

Sales processes need denied-party checks, end-use certificates where appropriate, and the power to refuse revenue.

Investors will ask about this after the first scare. Build the muscle earlier.

What to do

Classify your product against control lists with counsel. Document customer screening. Log decisions.

Pair with the export-controls-as-GTM memo: same risk family, different operational checklist.

  • US BIS: Export administration regulations and entity list primary.
  • UN Security Council sanctions information: International sanctions context; national implementation still binds firms.
