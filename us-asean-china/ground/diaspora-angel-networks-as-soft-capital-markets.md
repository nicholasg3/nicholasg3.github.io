# Ground brief — diaspora-angel-networks-as-soft-capital-markets

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** us-asean-china  
**Rank:** 27  
**Claim (particular):** Diaspora and regional angel networks function as **soft capital markets**: they move early cheques, diligence, and cross-border introductions using social trust where formal venture rails, local LP depth, or syndication norms are thin—adjacent to, but distinct from, pure remittance flows.

## Facts that must appear in the essay (with support)

1. **Remittances show scale of diaspora money rails—but are not the same as equity.** Philippines remittance inflows ~**$39 billion** in 2023 (World Bank Brief 40 top-five list). That is household finance and current-account support, not automatically startup equity.  
   Supports: World Bank Migration and Development Brief 40.

2. **Organized angel networks exist as durable institutions in SEA.** BANSEA positions itself as the region’s oldest angel investment network (founded 2001), with multi-country scope.  
   Supports: BANSEA public description.

3. **Diaspora equity vehicles are real and named when reported.** OV Ventures is covered as Vietnamese diaspora capital targeting domestic AI startups (2025 launch reporting).  
   Supports: Vietcetera.

4. **Angel networks lower coordination costs for small tickets.** Workshops, syndicates, and shared deal flow (AngelCentral model) are the mechanism, not a black-box “diaspora magic.”  
   Supports: AngelCentral site; SPF survey context.

## Theory hook

- **Social capital / dual embeddedness:** investors embedded in host financial markets and origin-country networks.
- **Informal institutions filling voids** when local VC density or exit markets are thin.
