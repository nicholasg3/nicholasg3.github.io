# Ground brief — chinese-platform-soft-power

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** us-asean-china  
**Claim (particular):** **Copying WeChat-like super-app bundling fails in Southeast Asia when national payments, identity, and regulatory rails do not match China’s single-stack environment.** Chinese platforms still influence templates and partnerships, but institutional complementarity—not soft power slogans—decides what bundles stick.

## Facts that must appear

1. **WeChat’s super-app completeness depends on China-specific rails** (single major market, WeChat Pay scale, mini-program developer stack).  
2. **SEA super apps (Grab, Gojek) grew from mobility and local payments across fragmented national markets**, not from cloning WeChat messaging as the daily OS.  
3. **Cross-border Chinese payment soft power often runs through partnerships (e.g. Alipay+ connect)** rather than replacing local wallets.  
4. **Regulatory and QR/payment fragmentation** is a binding constraint on “one app for ASEAN.”
