# Ground brief — data-localization-sea

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** us-asean-china  
**Claim (particular):** **Where personal and user data must live—and under what trigger—changes product architecture, vendor choice, and go-live sequencing more than brand localization does.** SEA is not one data-sovereignty regime.

## Facts that must appear

1. **Vietnam Decree 53/2022** elaborates the 2018 Cybersecurity Law; effective 1 Oct 2022; foreign enterprises providing listed services may be required to store specified user data in Vietnam and establish branch/rep office within 12 months of formal MPS request after defined triggers.  
   Supports: trade.gov summary.

2. **Captured service categories** include e-commerce, online payment, social media, ride-hailing connection, messaging, etc.  
   Supports: same.

3. **Indonesia Law 27/2022 (PDP)** is the comprehensive personal data law; cross-border transfers require equivalent protection, safeguards, or consent pathways; multiple secondary legal summaries state PDP Law itself lacks blanket localization, while sector rules (e.g. banks) can be stricter.  
   Supports: AWS Indonesia data privacy page; other legal overviews; official law PDF when available.

4. **Architecture implication:** multi-country SaaS with single global DB is a compliance bet, not a neutral default.
