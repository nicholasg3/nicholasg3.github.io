**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)

1. **DEFA is regional, late, and implementation-dependent.**  
   Supports: ASEAN AECC statement on substantial conclusion; expected signing by end-2026; language that benefits require full conclusion and effective implementation.

2. **Singapore already has operational bilateral/plurilateral digital agreements.**  
   Supports: Australia–Singapore DEA in force 8 Dec 2020 (DFAT); DEPA as first standalone digital trade agreement; Korea joined DEPA May 2024 (MTI).

3. **Founders ship against national implementation, not ASEAN press releases.**  
   Supports: Theory of incomplete contracts + national data/e-commerce rules; ERIA papers on data governance gaps if used carefully.

   Supports: ASEAN DEFA framing documents; treat as political target, not a binding schedule.
