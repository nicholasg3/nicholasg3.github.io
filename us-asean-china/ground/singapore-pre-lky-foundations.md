# Ground brief — singapore-pre-lky-foundations

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** us-asean-china  
**Claim (particular):** Singapore’s modern state capacity after 1965 built on inherited geography, free-port policy, legal-administrative rails, and entrepôt demographics that predated Lee Kuan Yew’s premiership. Leadership mattered; path dependence did too.

## Facts that must appear in the essay (with support)

1. **Modern Singapore’s colonial founding date is 6 February 1819 (Treaty of Singapore / trading-post establishment).**  
   Supports: Roots.sg founding story; NLB treaty article family; Wikisource 1819 Treaty text.

2. **The settlement was designed as a free port (no port duties as a core commercial rule), which drew regional merchants.**  
   Supports: NLB Raffles article (free-port policies); Roots.sg population growth to ~5,000 by 1821; Wikipedia founding years (secondary corroboration of free-port design).

3. **Singapore entered the Straits Settlements (with Penang and Malacca) in 1826, embedding British colonial administrative bundling.**  
   Supports: Roots.sg founding story.

4. **Independence as a sovereign republic is 9 August 1965 (separation from Malaysia).**  
   Supports: Standard official chronology (NLB / government commemorative materials). Leadership after 1965 is real; the claim is that port, law, and trade roles predate that break.

5. **British common-law and colonial administrative practice formed the legal base that independent Singapore adapted, not invented from zero.**  
   Supports: Roots.sg note that Crawfurd helped set up a legal system; Singapore’s legal system is widely described as common-law derived (use carefully; prefer heritage primaries over blog summaries).

## Theory hook

- **Path dependence / historical institutionalism:** early free-port and entrepôt choices raised the returns to openness, English commercial practice, and multi-ethnic trade networks.
- **Developmental state:** LKY-era state capacity is not denied; the brief rejects “great man alone created the city from jungle” compression.
