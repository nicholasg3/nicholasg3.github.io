# Ground brief — forest-city-network-school

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** us-asean-china  
**Claim (particular):** Forest City shows how a capital-heavy master plan can lose political legitimacy when buyer culture, immigration politics, and host-state control diverge. Network School’s later occupancy of that site made the collision between imported subculture and Malaysian licensing/immigration politics concrete.

## Facts that must appear (with support)

1. **Forest City launched mid-2010s by Country Garden (with Malaysian partners) as a large reclaimed-land project in Johor, often linked to BRI framing.**  
   Supports: BBC 2023; Business Times ASEAN coverage.

2. **By late 2023 BBC reporting: only ~15% of the project built; occupancy estimates just over 1% of total planned development; marketed heavily to Chinese buyers.**  
   Supports: BBC.

3. **2018 political shock: Mahathir restricted visas for Chinese buyers and framed the project as built for foreigners.**  
   Supports: BBC.

4. **Network School operated as a co-living / co-learning community in Forest City from 2024; public site states membership from $1,500/month including lodging/meals.**  
   Supports: ns.com.

5. **July 2026: Johor authorities revoked Network School’s business licence and ordered activities to cease (effective ~22 Jul 2026), after immigration-related controversy; council cited licensing/premises-use issues.**  
   Supports: Reuters / ST / TechNode Global; Star Malaysia on immigration probe finding no passport misuse (nuance).
