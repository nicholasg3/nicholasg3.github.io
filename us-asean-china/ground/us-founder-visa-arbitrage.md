## Facts that must appear

1. **Singapore EntrePass**: for foreign entrepreneurs starting/operating venture-backed or innovative-tech businesses (MOM).  
2. **Singapore Employment Pass**: minimum fixed monthly salary at least **S$5,600** (general) with COMPASS framework for new applicants (MOM work-pass framing).  
3. **US International Entrepreneur Rule**: discretionary parole; from 1 Oct 2024, qualified investment threshold **$311,071** or government award **$124,429**; initial parole up to 2.5 years, max 5 with re-parole; not a visa.  
4. **Implication:** incorporation and hiring geography respond to these thresholds.
