## Facts that must appear in the essay (with support)

1. **POFMA is a live administrative tool, not a paper statute.** POFMA Office continuously publishes ministerial instructions to issue Correction Directions (media centre shows ongoing use into 2025–2026).  
   Supports: https://www.pofmaoffice.gov.sg/media-centre/

2. **Mechanism is correction-first, not only deletion.** Typical POFMA correction path requires the recipient to publish a correction notice linking to government “correct facts” (Factually).  
   Supports: Factually.gov.sg; legal process summaries.

3. **Indonesia’s PSE registration created a binary operating gate.** In mid-2022, major platforms faced registration deadlines under Ministerial Regulation on private electronic system operators; non-registration risked blocking. Reuters and others documented the scramble and temporary blocks.  
   Supports: Reuters 2022 PSE coverage (canonical wire).

4. **Multi-country stacks multiply.** A regional product serving Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, and the Philippines inherits distinct speech, intermediary liability, and registration regimes.  
   Supports: comparative structure (POFMA + PSE + national computer-crime / intermediary laws) — avoid over-claiming uncited statutes.
