The hard part of running 11 AI agents was management

A status board reported that seven of 10 agents had failed. All were working. Across a year of running a small autonomous fleet, the costly failures were organizational rather than technical, and the fixes were job descriptions, an escalation clock and a limit on how much any agent may say.

What 11 AI agents are not allowed to do

A code-merging agent has been built, tested and scheduled, and has never merged anything, because a file it requires can only be created by a person. Autonomy in this fleet is granted by proof and prohibitions are enforced as missing capabilities.
