Fintech failure needs a resolution story before the outage

When a non-bank payments or crypto platform fails, customers discover whether funds were safeguarded, segregated, or merely promised. Resolution design is a product feature you write in peacetime.

The claim

Bank resolution frameworks exist because deposit-taking failure is a public problem. Many fintechs operate adjacent to that perimeter with lighter or different tools.

Users still expect bank-like safety. The gap between expectation and legal reality is the crisis narrative.

What to pre-define

Where customer funds sit (bank FBO accounts, trusts, exchange wallets). Who is the bankruptcy remote beneficiary. How long freezes last. Which customers get priority.

Public communications templates that do not invent deposit insurance.

Supervisory direction of travel

FSB and national authorities have published widely on non-bank financial intermediation and crypto-asset failure lessons after major platform collapses.

Read those as design constraints for safeguarding and disclosures, not as gotchas after launch.

What to do

Add a "Friday night failure" tabletop to every major product: who freezes what in 30 minutes, who posts the status page, who calls the bank partner.

If you cannot explain fund location in one plain paragraph, fix the structure before you scale marketing.

Source notes

  • Financial Stability Board: International primary hub for non-bank and crypto financial stability work.
  • BIS: Prudential and payments failure context.
  • FDIC: Deposit insurance boundary — what fintech claims must not imply if uninsured.
  • MAS: Example of a major hub regulator with explicit digital token and payments safeguarding themes.
