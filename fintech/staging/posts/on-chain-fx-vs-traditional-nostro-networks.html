On-chain FX still meets nostro reality

Atomic swaps and stablecoin corridors can move value quickly between wallets. Most real economy FX still settles through bank correspondent networks, liquidity providers, and local payout rails. Bridge design is the product.

The claim

Crypto-native FX demos show wallet-to-wallet conversion in seconds. Corporate and retail payouts still need local currency in a bank or cash-out network.

The hard product is the bridge: who provides liquidity, who takes FX risk, and which license covers the conversion.

Traditional stack

Nostro/vostro accounts, CLS for eligible currencies, and correspondent chains remain the institutional backbone for much cross-border settlement.

CPMI work on cross-border payments documents frictions that pure ledger transfers do not automatically solve: compliance, operating hours, and weak market infrastructure in some corridors.

Where on-chain helps

Treasury netting between crypto-native firms, certain remittance prefunding patterns, and always-on transfer between already-funded wallets.

Help is not the same as replacing the banking system for payroll taxes and supplier invoices in local currency.

What to do

Map each corridor as: on-chain segment + off-ramp entity + local license + liquidity provider. Missing any box means you do not have FX payments; you have a trading feature.

Price liquidity and compliance as COGS, not as surprises.
