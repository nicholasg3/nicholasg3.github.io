Zero commissions relocated the bill, not deleted it

Zero retail trading commissions changed market structure and incentive conflicts. The fee did not disappear; it moved.

The claim

Zero retail commissions did not make trading free. Revenue moved to payment for order flow, securities lending, interest on cash, and premium services.

Crypto and neobroker products repeating the zero-fee story should expect the same structural questions.

How to ground it

SEC market-structure materials and broker disclosures are the primary places to ground PFOF and best-execution debates.

Do not treat a marketing fee of $0 as proof of aligned incentives.

Operator consequences

When rates fall or regulation changes a revenue leg, platforms reprice users. Product strategy needs multiple revenue pillars.

What to do

In diligence, rebuild the revenue stack line by line. Ask which line is policy-sensitive.

If you copy zero-commission UX into new asset classes, copy the conflict-disclosure discipline too.

Source notes

  • SEC: US market structure and broker-dealer primary.
  • SEC Market Structure: Market structure data/materials hub.
  • FINRA: Broker-dealer conduct and disclosure context.
  • BIS: Cross-border market structure secondary.
