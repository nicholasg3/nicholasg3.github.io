In embedded finance, the complaint has many owners

When credit, accounts, or insurance sit inside a non-financial app, customers still experience one brand. Supervisors and courts care which legal entity failed. Complaint ownership is the product architecture.

The claim

Embedded finance puts regulated products inside software surfaces that do not look like banks. That improves distribution. It also multiplies the number of firms between a user problem and a fix.

If your partnership diagram cannot answer who the customer calls — and who must answer regulators — you have a brand, not a durable product.

Where duties live

Bank partners, program managers, lenders, insurers, and the platform each hold pieces of KYC, underwriting, servicing, and marketing rules.

Consumer protection agencies care about unfair practices and error resolution regardless of which logo was largest on the screen.

Contract design is UX

Service-level agreements for dispute timelines, data access for investigations, and wind-down plans when a partner exits are user experience under another name.

Opaque waterfall complaint handling produces social media escalations and supervisory attention.

What to do

Write a complaint RACI before launch: first response owner, decision owner, regulator contact owner, and public comms owner.

Test the path with a real ticket, not a slide. Time it. Fix the gaps that only appear in operations.
