Realtime payments compress fraud response time to zero

Instant settlement is a user benefit and a fraud acceleration. When push payments clear in seconds, recall windows shrink. Product design must put controls before the send button, not after the loss.

The claim

Batch and card systems often built delay and chargeback paths into the product. Realtime account-to-account rails optimize for speed and finality.

Fraudsters optimize for the same speed. Social-engineering scams that convince a user to authorize a push payment are especially hard to reverse.

What operators must redesign

Velocity limits, beneficiary confirmation, cooling-off for first-time payees, step-up authentication, and mule-account detection — in-flow, not only post-hoc SAR filing.

Customer support scripts matter as much as ML models. People under scam pressure need friction that is understandable.

Policy and rail context

FedNow materials and other RTP operator docs describe participation, settlement, and service features. They do not eliminate the need for bank-level fraud programs.

Compare consumer protection regimes carefully: card chargebacks are not a universal template for A2A push payments.

What to do

Before launching realtime send, write the fraud budget as a first-class P&L line and the control list as a launch gate.

Measure time-to-freeze and recovery rate, not only payment success rate.
