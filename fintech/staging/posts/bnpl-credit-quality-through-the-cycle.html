BNPL looks free until the cycle turns

Buy-now-pay-later products market smoothness at checkout. Credit quality, late fees, and household leverage show up later. Underwrite BNPL like consumer credit through the cycle, not like a payments feature.

The claim

BNPL compresses a credit decision into a purchase flow. That can expand access and conversion. It can also hide leverage from users who treat installments as not-debt.

Investors who model only take rates and GMV miss charge-offs, servicing costs, and funding sensitivity.

What to watch in primaries

Regulator reports and enforcement themes around disclosures, late fees, and credit reporting. Issuer filings for loss rates when public.

Cross-product stacking: multiple BNPL lines plus cards plus payday substitutes on the same household.

Through-the-cycle discipline

Growth cohorts underwritten in easy labor markets are not a strategy. Stress assumptions on unemployment and merchant category mix.

Merchant concentration can create correlated losses when one retail segment slows.

What to do

Separate payment processing economics from credit economics in every BNPL memo.

If you embed BNPL, negotiate information rights on losses and customer complaints — not only conversion lift.

Source notes

  • CFPB research reports: Search BNPL/consumer credit reports for primary supervisory research.
  • Federal Reserve consumer community context: Household credit condition framing.
  • BIS: Cross-country consumer finance / fintech credit secondary.
  • SEC EDGAR: Public BNPL/issuer filings for loss and revenue mix when available.
