## Facts that must appear

1. **SWIFT is a messaging cooperative (~11,000+ institutions, 200+ countries per SWIFT sanctions page framing)** — messaging, not settlement of value itself.
2. **12 March 2022:** SWIFT disconnected seven designated Russian entities (and designated Russia-based subsidiaries) under EU Council Regulation (EU) 2022/345.
3. **20 March 2022:** three Belarusian entities disconnected under 2022/398.
4. **2012 Iran precedent:** SWIFT announced it was instructed to discontinue services to sanctioned Iranian financial institutions after EU Council decision (15 Mar 2012 press).
5. **U.S. Treasury** imposed expansive sanctions on major Russian banks the same crisis window — complementary to, not identical with, SWIFT disconnect.
