# Ground brief — idle-asset-escheat-crypto

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** fintech  
**Claim (particular):** Exchange-held crypto is subject to ordinary state unclaimed-property (escheat) regimes. Dormancy, due-diligence notices, possible forced liquidation into USD, and multi-state holder compliance are real balance-sheet and customer-experience risks — not a crypto-only edge case.

## Facts that must appear

1. **US states impose dormancy periods (often ~1–5 years) after which holders must report and remit unclaimed property.**  
2. **Coinbase states it monitors inactivity, sends required notices, then transfers to the relevant state office.**  
3. **Some states require liquidation to USD; owners may reclaim only proceeds.**  
4. **RUUPA brought virtual currency into the property definition; states fill operational gaps differently.**  
5. **10-K language treats escheat of crypto (including airdrops/forks in some formulations) as material uncertainty.**
