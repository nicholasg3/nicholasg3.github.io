# Ground brief — tokenized-deposits-vs-stablecoins-liability-design

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** fintech  
**Claim (particular):** The binding difference between tokenized deposits and stablecoins is liability design — bank deposit claim settling toward central bank money versus a non-bank (or narrow-bank-like) bearer instrument on reserves — not which ledger software records the token. Garratt & Shin (BIS Bulletin 73) make the singleness-of-money case explicit.

## Who is arguing what

| Actor | Position | Source |
|-------|----------|--------|

## Facts that must appear

1. **Garratt & Shin key takeaway: private tokenised monies circulating as bearer instruments may trade away from par; tokenised deposits settling in CB money support singleness.**  
   Supports: BIS Bulletin 73.

2. **Both are liabilities of an issuer with a par redemption claim in the model — transfer process differs (bearer circulation vs account update + CB settlement).**  
   Supports: Bulletin 73 abstract/body.

3. **BIS 2025 AER: stablecoins offer programmability but perform poorly on singleness, elasticity, integrity as the monetary mainstay.**  
   Supports: BIS ar2025e3.

4. **Tokenized deposits remain commercial bank money inside the prudential perimeter when designed as deposits; stablecoin reserves are typically segregated assets, not insured bank deposits for the token holder.**  
   Supports: Bulletin 73 + standard banking framing; avoid inventing GENIUS Act detail unless citing primary statute carefully.
