**Claim (particular):** The Federal Reserve set U.S. reserve requirement ratios to zero effective 26 March 2020. That changed one legal ratio. It did not remove capital, liquidity, funding, or supervisory limits on bank credit.

## Theory hook

Credit-creation banking with **multiple binding constraints**: reserve ratios (historically), risk-weighted capital, LCR/NSFR-style liquidity, deposit funding cost, and supervisory judgment. Zero RR removes one lever; it does not create unconstrained credit.
