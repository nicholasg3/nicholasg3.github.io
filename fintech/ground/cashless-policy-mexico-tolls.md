# Ground brief — cashless-policy-mexico-tolls

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** fintech  
**Claim (particular):** Mexico’s federal push toward electronic toll collection (telepeaje / TAG) and reduced cash at CAPUFE plazas is a **cashless mandate at a physical choke point**. Official goals are speed and security; the operating risks are device access, system outages, exclusion of cash-only users, and surveillance of travel payments — inclusion rhetoric with infrastructure teeth.
