# Ground brief — mas-payments-licensing

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** fintech  
**Rank:** 6  
**Claim (particular):** An MAS payment-services licence signals fitness to operate in Singapore under the Payment Services Act 2019. It is not a passport that automatically authorizes the same activity across ASEAN or other markets.

## Facts that must appear

1. **Payment Services Act 2019** provides licensing of PSPs and oversight of payment systems; commenced **28 January 2020** (MAS payments page).
2. **Licence classes:** Standard Payment Institution (SPI), Major Payment Institution (MPI), money-changing — different thresholds.
3. **SPI thresholds examples:** S$3m monthly for one payment service (exclusions apply); S$6m for two+; S$5m daily outstanding e-money; base capital S$100,000; Singapore entity/branch requirements.
4. **MPI** for activity above thresholds / multiple services without those volume/float caps (per MAS page).
5. **Fit-and-proper, compliance, tech risk, audit** are assessment factors — licence is substantive supervision, not a sticker.

## Theory hook

Regulatory **home-host** problem: one supervisor’s license does not substitute for host-state authorization. Credibility signal ≠ passport.
