# Ground brief — on-chain-fx-vs-traditional-nostro-networks

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** fintech  
**Claim (particular):** On-chain or wCBDC FX experiments can speed matching and atomic settlement between tokens, but they do not erase nostro liquidity, corridor concentration, or last-mile cash constraints that still define cross-border money movement in correspondent banking.

## Who is arguing what

| Actor | Position | Source |
|-------|----------|--------|
| BIS Innovation Hub et al. (Project Mariana) | PoC: AMM-based trading/settlement of hypothetical EUR, SGD, CHF wholesale CBDCs; experimental, not policy commitment | BIS Project Mariana report (28 Sep 2023) |
| BIS CPMI | Cross-border payments remain slower/costlier/opaque; correspondent network shrinking while volumes rise | CPMI building blocks d193; correspondent banking data commentaries |
| Rice, von Peter, Boar (BIS QR) | Global retreat of correspondent banks | BIS Quarterly Review March 2020 |

## Facts that must appear

1. **FX market ~$7.5 trillion/day (Mariana framing).**  
   Supports: BIS Project Mariana page/report.

2. **Mariana: BIS IH + Banque de France + MAS + SNB tested AMM trading/settlement of hypothetical EUR, SGD, CHF wCBDCs; pure experiment.**  
   Supports: BIS othp75 / project page.

3. **Correspondent banking relationships declined sharply over the 2010s while payment volumes rose** — e.g. active relationships down ~20%+ over multi-year windows in CPMI commentaries; later updates cite ~29% decline over a decade with volume up.  
   Supports: BIS CPMI correspondent banking data / LinkedIn BIS summary of 2022 data; Rice et al. 2020.

4. **Traditional cross-border chains still rely on correspondent nostro/vostro balances and multi-hop messaging.**  
   Supports: CPMI cross-border payments roadmap context (d193 / d173 family).

## Theory hook

- **Correspondent banking / payment chains.**
- **Liquidity fragmentation and corridor economics.**
- **Atomic settlement ≠ complete money movement** when off-chain cash-out remains.
