# Ground brief — cbdc-retail-vs-wholesale

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** fintech  
**Rank:** 8  
**Claim (particular):** Retail (general-purpose) CBDC and wholesale CBDC answer different design problems. Retail debates touch public access, privacy, and cash complementarity. Wholesale debates touch interbank settlement finality among institutions.

## Who is arguing what

| Actor | Position | Source |
|-------|----------|--------|

| BIS payments primer (2020 QR) | Retail vs wholesale payment systems are a first distinguishing design feature | bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt2003f.htm |
| National CB projects (framing) | Many retail pilots; wholesale often RTGS modernization / tokenized settlement | cite group report, not invented pilots |

## Facts that must appear

1. **General-purpose CBDC = public day-to-day payments**; distinct from **wholesale** limited to financial-market / interbank payments (othp33).
2. **Foundational principles:** do not compromise monetary or financial stability; coexist with and complement existing money; promote innovation and efficiency.
3. **Contributing authorities:** Bank of Canada, ECB, BoJ, Riksbank, SNB, BoE, Fed Board, BIS.
4. **Retail systems have far more endpoints** than wholesale central-bank systems (report design discussion).
5. **Privacy and cash complementarity** sit mainly on the retail side of the design space; wholesale is closer to RTGS/settlement infrastructure.

## Theory hook

Public money hierarchy / two-tier banking: central bank money for public vs banks; settlement asset among institutions.
