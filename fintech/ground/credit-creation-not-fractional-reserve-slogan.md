# Ground brief — credit-creation-not-fractional-reserve-slogan

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** fintech  
**Claim (particular):** Modern bank money is created mainly when banks make loans (credit creation), not by “multiplying” a fixed pile of deposited cash through a simple fractional-reserve mechanical chain. Public education still leans on the 20th-century slogan; the binding constraints are capital, liquidity, risk appetite, and monetary policy — not a classroom reserve ratio (especially after US reserve requirements went to zero).

## Facts that must appear

1. **BoE 2014:** majority of money is bank deposits; principal way is commercial banks making loans; simultaneous deposit creation.  
2. **BoE rejects two misconceptions:** pure intermediation of pre-existing deposits; simple money-multiplier story from central bank reserves.  
3. **US reserve requirements = 0% since March 2020.**  
4. **Credit still faces capital, LCR/liquidity, funding cost, and supervision constraints.**  
5. **Fintech lenders that are not deposit-takers do not create bank deposits the same way** — platform credit vs bank credit is a liability-design distinction.
