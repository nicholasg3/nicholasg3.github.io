# Ground brief — fintech-failure-resolution-and-ring-fencing

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** fintech  
**Rank:** 25  
**Claim (particular):** When a payments or fintech group fails without clear **ring-fencing of client money** and a **resolution plan** that authorities can execute, the failure becomes a **political and supervisory event**, not only a private insolvency. Wirecard (2020) shows how missing cash, thin perimeter classification, and weak resolution readiness convert a technology-branded payments firm into a national scandal.

## Theory hook

- **Resolution regimes vs ordinary bankruptcy** (FSB Key Attributes).
- **Single point of entry (SPE) vs multiple point of entry (MPE)** and incentives to ring-fence.
- **Regulatory perimeter:** treating a payments group as “tech” vs “financial” changes resolution tools available.
