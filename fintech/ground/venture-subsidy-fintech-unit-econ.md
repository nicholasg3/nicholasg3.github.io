# Ground brief — venture-subsidy-fintech-unit-econ

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** fintech  
**Claim (particular):** Many fintech “efficient rail” stories only clear when customer acquisition, credit losses, and float are subsidized by cheap equity or credit cycles. Unit economics under venture subsidy are not the same product as unit economics under cost of capital at market rates — growth can mask negative contribution margins until funding tightens.
