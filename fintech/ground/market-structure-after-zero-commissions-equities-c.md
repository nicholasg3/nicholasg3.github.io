# Ground brief — market-structure-after-zero-commissions-equities-c

**Status:** grounded (2026-07-31)  
**Vertical:** fintech  
**Rank:** 28  
**Claim (particular):** **Zero-commission retail brokerage is a revenue-stack redesign**, not free trading. Payment for order flow (PFOF), securities lending, margin, cash sweeps, and crypto/options attachments replace ticket commissions — and that redesign carries **policy and conflict-of-interest risk**, as the SEC staff GameStop-era market structure report framed.

## Theory hook

- **Two-sided platforms / cross-subsidy:** free retail UX funded by wholesale order-flow buyers.
- **Market microstructure:** maker-taker, internalization, best execution.
- **Agency conflict:** broker as agent vs seller of order flow.
