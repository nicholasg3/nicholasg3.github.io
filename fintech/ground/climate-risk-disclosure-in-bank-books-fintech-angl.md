**Claim (particular):** **Climate risk disclosure in bank books becomes a fintech constraint through credit access and data demands.** As banks face TCFD/ISSB-aligned and Basel Pillar 3 climate disclosure pressure, they push **borrower- and counterparty-level climate data** down the chain — so BNPL books, SME lending platforms, and embedded credit fintechs inherit bank climate data and policy screens even when they never filed a TCFD report themselves.
