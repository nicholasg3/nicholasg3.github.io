ISA production wireframes

Every page the live ISA site needs to be functional, as a greybox wireframe. 17 pages across four flows. Each page names the wave-2 module that backs it.

Learner flow
  • Learner application — Can this person enter a cohort, and on what terms? [matching, verification]
  • Learner application status — Where is my application, and what does it need from me? [matching, verification]
  • ISA terms and transparency view — Exactly what will I pay, under which outcomes? [contracts, ledger, reporting]
  • ISA agreement — review and sign — What am I signing, and is this copy the binding one? [contracts, verification, ledger]
Employer flow
  • Requisition form — What role am I hiring for, and which cohorts can fill it? [matching]
  • Matched candidate list — Who fits this requisition, and how well? [matching, verification]
  • Interview pipeline — Where is every candidate, and what is blocking the next step? [matching, reporting]
  • Milestone fee schedule — What do I owe, when, and what fired it? [ledger, contracts]
Provider flow
  • Cohort creation — What am I running, when, and on which ISA terms? [contracts, matching]
  • Seat inventory — How many seats are sold, held, or at risk across my cohorts? [matching, ledger]
  • Outcome reporting — What happened to my graduates, and can I prove it? [reporting, verification]
  • Success fees — What has the ISA book earned me, and when is it paid? [ledger, reporting]
Ops flow
  • Ops dashboard — What is the book doing, and what needs a human today? [reporting, ledger, verification, matching]
  • Verification queue — Which claims are unproven, and what evidence closes them? [verification]
  • Dispute cases — What is contested, by whom, and what is the clock? [verification, contracts, ledger]
  • Ledger view — Where did every pound come from and go, and does it balance? [ledger]
  • Funder dashboard — Is the portfolio performing against what the funder was promised? [reporting, ledger]
Wave-2 modules referenced
  • matching — requisitions, candidate scoring, seat assignment
  • contracts — terms templates, agreement generation, signature, clauses
  • ledger — append-only money events, collections, payouts, reconciliation
  • verification — identity, income, employment and completion claims
  • reporting — outcome reports, transparency figures, funder reporting
Greybox wireframes. No visual design, no copy, no live data.
